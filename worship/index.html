Songs from the life of the local church

Bread From Heaven Worship is the worship band of Bread From Heaven Christian Fellowship. These songs are written and arranged to help the church behold Christ, proclaim His gospel, and respond together in joyful, Scripture-rooted worship.

Our songs seek to uphold the Reformed doctrines we confess as a church, with lyrics shaped by Scripture and tested for doctrinal faithfulness. We aim to be content first and music next, letting melody and arrangement serve the truth being sung.
